## 👨‍⚕️ About Me

I am **Rahul M. Varman, MD**, a Facial Plastic and Reconstructive Surgeon, Otolaryngologist, and Assistant Clinical Professor with a strong background in surgical innovation, academic medicine, and clinical research.

My professional journey combines advanced surgical training, evidence-based clinical practice, interdisciplinary collaboration, and a commitment to improving patient outcomes.

My clinical and academic interests span facial plastic and reconstructive surgery, facial nerve disorders, craniofacial surgery, rhinoplasty, head and neck oncology, and medical education.

Beyond clinical practice, I am interested in the evolving intersection of **Medicine, Healthcare Technology, Artificial Intelligence, and Clinical Innovation**. I believe technology and data-driven approaches can create meaningful opportunities to improve clinical workflows, healthcare delivery, and patient-centered outcomes.
